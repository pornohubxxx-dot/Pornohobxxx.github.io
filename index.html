1. Before using an earning app

Check whether the service accepts users in Kenya, its minimum withdrawal amount, payment methods, fees and recent independent reviews. Never assume that advertised earnings are guaranteed.

2. Learn a digital skill

Choose one skill, use free tutorials, practise consistently and create examples of your work. You can then look for genuine paid projects. Results depend on your skill, effort and demand.

3. Understand affiliate marketing

An affiliate programme gives approved members referral links. You may earn a commission when someone completes a qualifying action through your link. Read the terms and clearly disclose affiliate relationships to your visitors.

4. Start an online project

Start with a useful topic, publish original content and make the site easy to use on mobile phones. Build trust and attract visitors before expecting advertising or affiliate income.

5. Protect yourself from online scams

Be careful with guaranteed-profit claims, pressure to deposit money, unexplained withdrawal fees and requests for passwords or verification codes. Verify businesses independently before signing up.

6. Look for your first client

Build two or three sample projects that demonstrate your ability. Describe your service clearly, agree on payment terms before starting work, and use reputable channels to find potential clients.
